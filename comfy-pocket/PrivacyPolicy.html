Privacy Policy

Comfy Pocket · Effective: November 26, 2026

Data we collect

The developer of Comfy Pocket (“the app”) does not collect any personal information or usage data. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Data sent to the servers you add

The app sends the following directly to the ComfyUI servers you add. Nothing passes through the developer's servers.

  • Workflows, prompts, numbers and other inputs
  • Images you choose
  • Your authentication token (only if you set one)

How that data is handled is up to the operator of each server, usually you.

Data stored on your device

Server addresses, workflows, input drafts, run history and results you save stay on your device only. Authentication tokens and app lock passcodes are stored in the device Keychain. Deleting the app removes this data.

Photos and local network

Photo Library access is used only to add results when you save them. Local Network access is used to connect to ComfyUI on your network.

Sharing with third parties

Because the developer collects no data, none is shared with third parties.
